Amateur radio training

Bench Test

Interactive pages for the technical part of the amateur radio syllabus. The circuits are solved in the browser as you change them.

Each page turns the circuits from a syllabus group into working instruments. There are controls for the component values. Move one and the page solves the circuit again: the readings update, the waveforms redraw, the working underneath substitutes the new values, and the charge moving round the schematic changes speed to match the current in each branch.

Everything on the page is calculated as you move the control, from a nodal analysis of the circuit. The pages are free, run entirely in the browser, and work offline once loaded.

They are meant to sit alongside a course and the books, for the reader who wants to see a circuit respond before the idea settles. They are used with the course at course.g0lft.org.

The pages

Groups 2A to 2C

Resistance, power and tolerance

Ohm’s law and power, series and parallel networks, potential dividers, source resistance and tolerance, from a first circuit at Foundation to the worst case at Full.

Foundation, Intermediate, Full

Group 2D

Reactive components

What sets a capacitance and an inductance, series and parallel combinations, dielectric losses, polarised capacitors and breakdown, induction, back EMF and the flyback diode, and the CR and LR time constants.

Intermediate, Full

Group 2E

AC theory

Frequency and RMS, lead and lag, reactance and impedance, coupling and decoupling, and wavelength, with voltage and current traced against time.

Foundation, Intermediate, Full

Group 2F

Sampling

Converters, quantisation, sample rate and aliasing, from what a converter does at Foundation to aliasing and the Fourier transform at Full.

Foundation, Intermediate, Full

Group 2H

Tuned circuits

Resonance, Q, bandwidth, dynamic resistance and the four filter shapes, with the circulating current visible in the tank.

Intermediate, Full

Group 2I

Semiconductors

Diodes, Zeners, varicaps, transistor characteristics and biasing, the three configurations, amplifier classes and oscillators.

Intermediate, Full

Group 2J

Power supplies

Cells and batteries, then half wave, centre tap and bridge rectifiers stepped through time with real diodes: the ripple, the switch-on surge, the reverse voltage on each diode, Zener and error amplifier stabilisers dropping out, and why a switch mode supply needs a smaller capacitor.

Foundation, Intermediate, Full

Group 3F

RF power amplifiers

Efficiency and the matched load, linearity and intermodulation, the pi network as match and filter, peak and average power, and ALC.

Foundation, Intermediate, Full

Group 3I

The superhet receiver

The mixer and everything it makes, the local oscillator above or below, the image and how much the RF stage cuts it, high and low IFs and the double superhet, and the IF transformer as two coupled tuned circuits, under, critical and over-coupled.

Intermediate, Full

Groups 4A, 4B, 4E, 4F

Feeders, SWR and matching

Feeder loss, characteristic impedance and velocity factor, stubs, baluns, standing waves, SWR and return loss, what feeder loss hides, T, pi and L matching units, and the quarter-wave transformer, each on a real length of lossy feeder.

Foundation, Intermediate, Full

Each page covers every level that teaches its group, or groups. The level buttons at the top switch the whole page, so the same instruments appear with the depth, the equations and the worked answers set for that level.
